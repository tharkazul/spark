# Privacy Policy for Spark (Spark APP)

**Last Updated:** August 6, 2026

Spark ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Spark mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is capable of being associated with you ("Personal Data").

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, password hash, profile picture, and account settings.
- **Physical & Health Metrics:** Body weight, height, body fat percentage, target goals, workout preferences, and daily fitness logs.
- **Physique Photos:** Progress photos you explicitly choose to upload to monitor your physical transformation.
- **AI Coach Interactions:** Messages, queries, and feedback submitted to the Spark AI Coach.

### B. Information Synchronized from Third-Party Services
If you connect third-party fitness platforms (e.g., Strava, Garmin Connect, Apple Health, Google Health Connect), we collect:
- Workout activities (distance, duration, pace, elevation, exercise types).
- Physiological metrics (heart rate, calories burned, power metrics, recovery indicators).
- API access tokens required to perform automated syncs on your behalf.

### C. Automatically Collected Information
- **Device & Usage Data:** Operating system version, device model, app version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and interaction logs for crash reporting and app performance optimization.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your Personal Data to provide, improve, and personalize the Spark experience, specifically to:
1. **Deliver Core Features:** Calculate your personalized Spark recovery scores, fitness progression, and training load.
2. **Provide AI Coaching:** Process chat inputs via artificial intelligence models (Google Gemini API) to generate personalized fitness advice, workout recommendations, and nutrition summaries.
3. **Third-Party Integration:** Synchronize workouts and health metrics seamlessly with Strava and Garmin Connect.
4. **Community & Leaderboards:** Display opt-in gamification scores and public profile metrics to fellow Spark users if enabled.
5. **Security & Performance:** Detect, prevent, and troubleshoot technical errors or fraudulent activity.

---

## 3. How We Share & Disclose Information

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose your information only in the following contexts:

- **AI Service Providers:** Text inputs submitted to the Spark AI Coach are processed via Google Cloud / Gemini API under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements. Inputs are not used to train public foundation models without user consent.
- **Connected Third-Party APIs:** When you connect Strava or Garmin, data is exchanged in accordance with their respective developer agreements and your authorization.
- **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

---

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

- We retain your Personal Data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the App services.
- **Account Deletion:** You have the right to request full deletion of your account and all associated personal data (including physical metrics, chat histories, connected tokens, and physique photos) at any time.
  - **In-App Deletion:** Navigate to **Settings > Account > Delete Account**.
  - **Web Request:** Visit our web deletion request page or contact us at **privacy@sparkapp.fitness**. Upon request, all personal data is permanently purged from our active databases within 30 days.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal data, including AES-256 encryption for stored OAuth tokens and HTTPS/TLS transport security for all data transfers.

---

## 7. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:
- **Access & Portability:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data.
- **Withdrawal of Consent:** Disconnect Strava, Garmin, or third-party integrations at any time via App Settings.
