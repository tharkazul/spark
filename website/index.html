Training that bends when your week does.

rooka is an AI coach for runners, cyclists, triathletes and hybrid athletes. It plans your season back from your goal, fits each week to the hours you actually have, and reworks the plan when you’re tired, travelling or carrying a niggle.

Your season, planned back from race day.

Set a race and rooka lays out Base, Build, Peak and Taper. Training for health or body composition instead? You get Adapt, Develop, Crunch and Sustain. Either way, every week has a job.

minutes × zone = rooka

Every session gets a rooka score: the time you spent multiplied by the heart-rate zone you spent it in. A long easy ride and a short interval session can land on the same number. rooka adds them up day by day to track three things.

Fitness

Your load averaged over the last 42 days. It climbs slowly, and that slow climb is the base you race on.

Fatigue

Your load averaged over the last 7 days. It jumps after a big week and drops fast when you back off.

Form

Fitness minus fatigue. Negative while you’re building, positive when you’re fresh. The taper is timed so it peaks on race morning.

When the week doesn’t go to plan.

Tell rooka how many minutes you have each day and which club sessions are fixed. It fills in the rest. When something changes, tap Adapt on today’s session:

  • Time crunchShorten the session and keep the part that matters.
  • Move indoorsRewrite it for the trainer or treadmill.
  • Move all one dayPush the whole schedule back 24 hours.
  • Cancel completelyRest today. The rest of the week rebalances.

Going away? Tell the coach about a trip and it remembers. Every plan it writes for those dates works around it.

Something hurts? Log a niggle and the plan changes with it. A sore calf swaps hill repeats for cycling. A sore knee pauses heavy squats.

A coach you can just talk to

The coach sees your plan, your synced workouts and how you’ve been recovering, so you can ask the question you’d ask a person.

  • Ask what’s on today, why, and what to change if it’s pouring outside.
  • Send a photo of your meal and it logs it against today’s targets.
  • Get a debrief after each synced workout.
  • Chat in English, Dutch, German, French or Spanish.

Tell it where it hurts

Tap the part of the body that’s bothering you and rate how bad it is. The coach changes your plan straight away and shows you exactly what it changed.

  • A body map that shows which areas are fresh, fatigued or injured.
  • A 7-day muscle workload model across quads, calves, hamstrings, glutes, core and upper body.
  • Resting heart rate and HRV from Apple Health, once connected.
  • Optional cycle tracking that adjusts training volume and intensity by phase.

See what your training is building

Fitness, fatigue and form charts show where you are in the season. An archetype radar shows what kind of athlete your training is making you.

  • Five axes: endurance, strength, explosiveness, versatility and consistency.
  • Benchmark tests to set your FTP, swim CSS and threshold heart rate.
  • Heart-rate zones per sport, which you can edit.
  • Weight and body composition tracking.

Questions

Which sports does rooka plan for?

Running, cycling, swimming and strength training, and any mix of them. That covers triathlon, Hyrox and other hybrid goals. You can also add fixed sessions like a weekly club run or football practice, and rooka schedules around them.

Which watches work with it?

Garmin watches that sync with Garmin Connect, Suunto watches, and Apple Watch with an iPhone on iOS 17 or later. All three can receive structured workouts. Other watches can still feed in workouts through Apple Health or Strava.

How do I install the beta on iPhone?

Install Apple’s free TestFlight app from the App Store, then request an invite above using your Apple ID email. When the invite arrives, open it on your iPhone and tap Install.

How do I install the beta on Android?

Request an invite above using the email address of your Google account. We’ll add you to the Google Play test and send a link. Open it on your phone, accept the invitation and install rooka from the Play Store.

Which AI does rooka use, and what happens to my data?

The coach runs on Google Gemini through Google’s paid API. Under those terms Google doesn’t use your requests to train its models. We only send what the coach needs to answer, such as your recent training, readiness and your question.

We don’t sell your data. Connection tokens and Garmin login details are stored encrypted with AES-256, and the app talks to our servers over HTTPS. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is rooka medical advice?

No. rooka gives fitness guidance only. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t diagnose anything. Check with a doctor before starting hard training, and stop if something hurts sharply.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Account, then Delete Account & Purge Data. You can also email privacy@rooka.io from the address on your account. Everything is removed within 30 days.
