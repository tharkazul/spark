Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. Applies to the rooka app and rooka.io.

rooka (“we”, “us”) runs the rooka app for iPhone and Android and the website rooka.io. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it and how to delete it. If you don’t agree with it, please don’t use rooka.

1. What we collect

Things you give us

  • Account details: name, username, email address and a hashed password. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email they share with us.
  • Athlete profile: age, sex, weight, height, goals and race dates, weekly availability, recurring sessions, heart-rate zones and benchmark results such as FTP or swim CSS.
  • Health and wellbeing entries: sleep quality, fatigue, soreness and niggles you log, and body measurements such as weight and body fat.
  • Cycle tracking: if you turn it on, the period start dates you log and the cycle phase we work out from them.
  • Coach chat: the messages you send, any photos you attach (such as meal photos) and voice notes you record. Voice notes are turned into text; we don’t keep the recording.
  • Home town: if you set one, the town’s name and approximate coordinates, used for your weather forecast. The app never uses your phone’s location.

Things you let us bring in from other services

  • Garmin Connect: activities, daily health data such as HRV and sleep, and live workout data if you use live coaching. Section 4 explains exactly what we receive from Garmin and what we do with it.
  • Suunto: workouts with heart rate, distance and elevation, through Suunto’s official API. With your permission we also send planned workouts to your watch as SuuntoPlus Guides.
  • Apple Health: workouts and their distances, heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep stages, steps, active energy, weight, body fat and VO2 max, as you allow in iOS Health settings.
  • Strava: your activities and their details (distance, time, pace, heart rate, power, elevation). With your permission we also write a workout summary into the activity’s title or description on Strava.

Things collected automatically

  • Whether you use an iPhone or an Android phone.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • Technical logs on our servers, such as errors and when requests were made, used to fix problems. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

2. How we use it

  • To plan your season and weekly sessions and change them when you use Adapt, report a niggle or tell the coach about a trip.
  • To calculate your rooka training load, fitness, fatigue, form, readiness and muscle workload.
  • To answer you in coach chat, analyse your synced workouts and set nutrition targets.
  • To show your sessions, quests, points and leaderboard position to the friends you connect with.
  • To send structured workouts to your Apple Watch, Garmin or Suunto watch when you ask.
  • To show the weather forecast for your home town and let the coach take it into account.
  • To manage your subscription and send account emails such as password resets.

We don’t use your data for advertising and we don’t sell it.

3. AI transparency statement

rooka’s coach uses artificial intelligence: Google’s Gemini API. It only runs after you’ve said yes, and you can switch it off at any time.

What the AI does. It answers your messages in coach chat, plans and adjusts your training, analyses your workouts, sets nutrition targets, creates quests and writes check-in messages.

Your consent comes first. The first time you open the coach, the app asks whether you allow this. Until you tap Allow, nothing about you is sent to Google.

What is sent. If you allow it, then for each of those tasks we send Google the data needed for that task. That can include:

  • your messages to the coach, and photos and voice notes you send it;
  • health and fitness data, such as workouts, heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps, active energy, weight and VO2 max, including data from Garmin, Apple Health and the other services you connect;
  • your athlete profile, goals, plan, niggles and the training load and readiness rooka calculates.

No training on your data. Google processes this on our behalf only to produce the coach’s answer. We use Google’s paid API, under whose terms Google doesn’t use these requests to train its models. We never use your data, including data from Garmin or anything derived from it, to train, test or improve any AI model.

Withdrawing consent. You can turn the AI coach off at any time in the app under Profile, then AI coach. From then on nothing is sent to Google, and the AI features (coach chat, plan adjustments, quests, nutrition targets and coach messages) stay off until you turn it back on. Syncing, scores and friends keep working.

4. Data from Garmin

If you connect your Garmin account, this is what happens with your Garmin data.

What we receive and send

  • From Garmin: your activities, both summaries and the full activity files (time, distance, route, heart rate, pace, power, cadence and laps), daily health data such as HRV, sleep, resting heart rate and stress, and menstrual cycle data if you choose to share it in Garmin Connect.
  • To Garmin: the structured workouts rooka plans for you, so they appear in your Garmin Connect calendar and on your watch.
  • Live coaching (optional): if you add your personal rooka address as a LiveTrack contact in Garmin Connect, Garmin emails us your LiveTrack link when you start a workout. While the workout runs we read your live heart rate, pace, power and position from it to send coaching cues to your watch. Positions stay in memory only. We keep the effort numbers, the cues we sent and a short summary, and we delete the LiveTrack link when the workout ends.

How you connect. We’re moving to Garmin’s official connection, where you sign in on Garmin’s own page, choose what to share, and rooka never sees your Garmin password. Until your account uses it, rooka connects with the Garmin username and password you gave us, stored encrypted. Once you switch, we delete that stored login.

What we use it for

  • Logging your workouts and comparing them with what was planned.
  • Calculating your training load, fitness, fatigue, form and readiness, and adjusting the coming days of your plan.
  • Showing you your training history, charts and progress, and showing your activities and training load to the connections you accept on rooka (see section 5).
  • The AI coach, only if you’ve allowed it (see section 3).

Who processes it

Garmin data is processed only by the providers that run rooka for us, listed in section 5: Google Cloud (our servers and backups), Cloudflare (network traffic and LiveTrack emails), Google’s Gemini API (only if you allow the AI coach), Expo, Apple and Google (push notifications, which can mention a workout) and Google Workspace (the weekly summary email).

We don’t sell Garmin data and we don’t share it with anyone for their own use. We don’t use it, or anything derived from it, to train, test or improve AI models.

Stopping it

You can disconnect Garmin in the app, or remove rooka’s access in your Garmin Connect account settings, at any time. Either way, rooka stops receiving Garmin data and sending workouts to Garmin, and we delete your stored Garmin login or connection tokens. Workouts already synced stay in your training history until you delete them, or your account (see section 8).

5. Who else sees your data

We don’t sell your data. The providers below process it only on our behalf, to run rooka for you.

  • Services you connect: Garmin, Suunto, Apple Health and Strava, under their own terms and only as far as you authorise.
  • Google Cloud: hosts our servers and database, where your data, including data from Garmin, is stored, and our daily backups.
  • Cloudflare: carries traffic between the app and our servers, and forwards the Garmin LiveTrack emails used for live coaching.
  • Google (Gemini API): AI processing, only if you allow it, as described in section 3.
  • Expo, Apple and Google: deliver push notifications to your phone and watch. A notification can mention a workout or a coaching cue.
  • Google Workspace: sends account emails, such as password resets, and the weekly training summary email.
  • Apple WeatherKit: to get your forecast, our server sends Apple the area around your home town, rounded to about 10 km, without your name or any account details.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when you search for your home town, our server sends the text you typed to OpenStreetMap’s place search, without your name or any account details.
  • Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat: subscription purchases and status. We never see your card details.
  • Your connections on rooka: people you have accepted as connections see your activities, training load, points, level and leaderboard position. Other rooka users who find you by username only see your username, profile picture, level and title. You can remove a connection at any time from their profile.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

6. The website

rooka.io doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. It stores your light or dark mode choice in your browser. Fonts load from Google Fonts. When you request a beta invite or contact support, the form sends your email address (and message) to us through FormSubmit, and we keep beta requests on our server so we can send invites.

7. Security

Connection tokens and Garmin login details are encrypted with AES-256. Passwords are stored as hashes. The app talks to our servers over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit who can access your data and what we keep.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete it at any time:

  • In the app: Profile, then Account, then Delete Account & Purge Data.
  • By email: write to privacy@rooka.io from the address on your account with the subject “Account deletion request”.

Deleting your account removes your profile, training history, chat, photos and connection details. Copies in our daily backups expire within 14 days, so everything is gone within 30 days at the latest. You can also delete single workouts, including ones synced from Garmin, in the app. Disconnecting Garmin in the app removes your stored Garmin login or connection tokens straight away. Disconnecting Strava revokes rooka’s access on Strava’s side.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and, if you’re in California, the CCPA) you can ask to see a copy of your data, correct it, move it to another service (export), restrict or object to how we use it, or have it deleted. This includes data we received from Garmin. You can correct your profile yourself in the app, and delete workouts or your whole account there too (see section 8).

You can withdraw consent at any time: turn off the AI coach in the app (section 3), disconnect Garmin or another service in the app or in that service’s own settings, or turn off Apple Health access in iOS Settings.

For a copy or export of your data, or anything else, email privacy@rooka.io and we’ll respond within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands that’s the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

10. Not medical advice

rooka is not a medical device and doesn’t diagnose or treat anything. Readiness scores, plans, nutrition targets and coach answers are general fitness guidance. Talk to a doctor before starting hard training or changing your diet.

11. Children

rooka is for people aged 16 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from younger children.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll update the date at the top and let you know before the change applies.

13. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@rooka.io
Everything else: support@rooka.io
