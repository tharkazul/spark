Spark App Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Spark ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via the Spark application and associated services, the privacy and security of our users' health and personal data is our highest priority.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide personalized athletic training plans, recovery diagnostics, and AI coaching:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, and user preferences.
  • Health & Activity Data: Workout sessions (running, cycling, swimming, strength), heart rate, duration, distance, elevation, and training load.
  • Integrations: With your explicit authorization, data synced from Apple HealthKit, Garmin Connect, and Strava.

2. Apple HealthKit & WorkoutKit Data

Spark adheres strictly to Apple's HealthKit guidelines:

  • HealthKit data is used exclusively to provide fitness coaching and schedule structured workouts on your Apple Watch.
  • We never sell your HealthKit data to third parties, advertising platforms, or data brokers.
  • We do not use HealthKit data for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate adaptive AI workouts and training volume recommendations.
  • To calculate Spark XP, recovery metrics, and workout readiness scores.
  • To sync scheduled workouts directly to your connected fitness devices.

4. Data Storage and Security

All sensitive credentials and authentication tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Communications between the app and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS / SSL/TLS.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may disconnect integrations (Apple Health, Garmin, Strava) or request complete deletion of your account and all associated workout logs at any time via the Settings screen in the app.
