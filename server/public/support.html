Support

Setting up the beta, connecting your watch, and managing your account.

Contact us

Email support@rooka.io, or use the form below. For privacy and data requests, write to privacy@rooka.io.

Installing the beta

iPhone

  • Install Apple’s free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  • Request an invite on the homepage with your Apple ID email.
  • Open the invite email on your iPhone, or open TestFlight, and tap Install.

Android

  • Request an invite on the homepage with the email of the Google account on your phone.
  • When we’ve added you, open the testing link we send, accept the invitation and install rooka from the Play Store.

Then open rooka, sign in with Apple, Google or email, and the setup chat walks you through your goal, weekly hours and connections.

Connecting your watch and apps

All connections live under Profile, then Connections.

Apple Watch and Apple Health

Tap Connect Watch and allow access in the iOS Health prompt. rooka reads workouts, heart rate, sleep and HRV. To put a planned session on your wrist, open its menu and tap Send to device. It appears in the Workout app on your Apple Watch with its steps and targets. Sending workouts needs an iPhone on iOS 17 or later.

Garmin

Tap Connect Garmin and enter your Garmin Connect email and password. rooka imports your workouts and HRV and puts planned sessions on your Garmin Connect calendar. Your watch or Edge picks them up on its next sync.

Suunto

Tap Connect next to Suunto and sign in to your Suunto account to approve access. rooka imports your workouts, and planned sessions you send with Send to device appear on your watch as SuuntoPlus Guides.

Strava

Tap Connect with Strava and approve access. New activities come in automatically and rooka adds a workout summary to them on Strava. rooka+ members can choose whether that summary includes the rooka.io credit.

Subscription

rooka+ costs €6.99 a month or €69.99 a year and starts with a 14-day free trial. It’s billed by Apple or Google, so you manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions. You’ll find a shortcut under Profile, then Account.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile, then Account, then Delete Account & Purge Data. Or email privacy@rooka.io from the address on your account with the subject “Account deletion request”. Everything is removed within 30 days. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, so cancel that separately.
