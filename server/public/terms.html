1. What rooka is

rooka is an AI-assisted training app. It plans training, calculates training load, readiness and muscle workload, logs workouts and nutrition, and answers questions through a chat coach powered by generative AI. It can connect to Garmin Connect, Suunto, Apple Health and Strava.

2. Health disclaimer

rooka is not a medical device or a healthcare provider. Plans, readiness scores, nutrition targets and coach answers are general fitness guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Exercise carries a risk of injury and strain on your heart and body. You train at your own risk. Check with a doctor before starting hard training or changing your diet, and stop if anything hurts sharply or feels wrong.

3. AI answers can be wrong

The coach uses Google’s Gemini models. It’s built around training principles, but its answers can contain mistakes. Use your own judgement and never do a session that feels unsafe.
