# Running Form & Biomechanics Training Guidelines 🏃

## Core Coaching Directives
- **Microscopic Granularity**: Avoid generic descriptions like "intervals" or "easy run". Every single session's `details` must include explicit cadence cues, biomechanical form focus, running drill warm-ups, and pacing targets.
- **Biomechanical Efficiency**: Focus on compact arm swing, tall posture, light foot strike under center of mass, and rapid heel recovery ("high heels").
- **Session Structure**: Every session's `details` must specify:
  1. *Dynamic Warm-up & Drills*: Specific dynamic mobility and running technique drills.
  2. *Primary Work*: Prescribed intervals/distance, target heart rate/pace, cadence cues (spm).
  3. *Post-Run Neuromuscular Work*: Strides or mobility to reinforce neural firing.
  4. *Cool-down & Recovery*: Active flushing, calf/hamstring mobility, hydration.

## Prescribed Drills & Running Mechanics

### 1. Pre-Run Dynamic Mobility & Form Drills
- *Dynamic Mobility*: 2 x 10 Leg swings (front-to-back and lateral), 2 x 15 ankle dorsiflexion rocks, walking knee hugs, walking quad pull with reach.
- *Technique Drills*: 
  - 3 x 30m A-Skips: Driving knee up with ankle flexed, snapping foot down under hips.
  - 3 x 30m Butt Kicks / Heel Recovery Drills: Rapid heel pull directly under glutes ("high heels" cue) without leaning forward.
  - 3 x 30m B-Skips: Active leg extension and paw-back ground strike.
  - 4 x 60m relaxed Strides building to 90% top speed with full walk-back recovery.

### 2. Form & Biomechanical Cues to Specify in Session Details
- *Cadence Control*: Prescribe maintaining 175–185 spm to reduce ground contact time and joint impact forces.
- *Heel Recovery / Knee Drive*: Cue "pull heel high to hamstring" ("high heels") for faster swing phase and knee clearance.
- *Posture & Foot Strike*: Cue "run tall, slight forward lean from ankles (not hips), midfoot strike directly beneath center of mass".
- *Breathing & Pacing Rhythm*: Structured breathing patterns (e.g. 3:3 nasal or rhythmic 2:2 breathing on threshold efforts).
