# Triathlon & Ironman Training & Drill Guidelines 🏊‍♀️🚴‍♀️🏃‍♀️

## Core Coaching Directives
- **Microscopic Granularity**: Avoid generic descriptions like "45-min swim drill", "easy recovery ride", or "tempo intervals". Every single session's `details` must include explicit interval breakdowns, equipment usage, drills, cadences, wattages/zones, and paces.
- **Technique & Biomechanical Focus**: Emphasize economy of motion, neuromuscular efficiency, and transition adaptation across all three disciplines.
- **Session Structure**: Every session's `details` must outline:
  1. *Warm-up*: Progressive activation with dedicated technique drills.
  2. *Main Technique & Work Set*: Explicit intervals, equipment (pull buoy, paddles, fins, kickboard, ERG mode, aero bars), cadence (rpm/spm), and target zones/paces.
  3. *Cool-down*: Active flushing and technique reset.
  4. *Fueling Strategy*: Specific intra-workout hydration, carbohydrate (g/hr), and electrolyte targets.

## Prescribed Drills & Discipline Guidelines

### 1. Swim Drills & Equipment
- *Equipment Usage*: Actively prescribe pull buoy, hand paddles, fins, kickboard, and snorkel to isolate technique.
- *Catch-Up Drill*: 4 x 50m holding a float or touching lead hand before initiating catch, with 15s rest, to eliminate premature pull and drill front-quadrant swimming.
- *EVF (Early Vertical Forearm) Technique*: 8 x 50m with pull buoy and hand paddles holding CSS + 2s/100m pace, focusing on high elbows and initiating the pull from the fingertips down.
- *Hypoxic Tolerance & Race Simulation*: 4 x 100m breathing bilaterally every 5th stroke to build lung tolerance under race stress.
- *Open-Water Sighting Drill*: 200m pool drill lifting eyes forward twice per 25m during the stroke without dropping the hips or disturbing stroke rhythm.

### 2. Bike Drills & Cadence Work
- *Over-Under Intervals*: E.g., 3 sets of 3 x (2 mins at 95% FTP / 85 rpm + 1 min at 105% FTP / 95 rpm standing climb), with 4 mins recovery between sets, training lactate clearance.
- *Single-Leg Pedaling Drills*: 4 x (45s right leg only, 15s switch, 45s left leg only, 15s switch, 60s both legs at 100 rpm smooth cadence) to eliminate dead spots at 12 and 6 o'clock in the pedal stroke.
- *Aero-Position Adaptation*: Zone 2/3 endurance ride with the final 20–30 minutes strictly locked in aerodynamic aero bars at 90 rpm without breaking posture.
- *High-Cadence Spin-Ups*: 5 x 30s progressive spin-ups reaching 110–120 rpm in Zone 2 to promote neuromuscular pedal stroke efficiency.

### 3. Run Drills & Brick Transitions
- *Running Form Drills*: 3 x 30m high knees into 3 x 30m butt kicks into 3 x 30m A-skips and B-skips prior to any tempo or interval session, cueing rapid heel recovery ("high heels") and tall posture.
- *Neuromuscular Strides*: 6 x 80m relaxed strides on grass or track focusing on midfoot strike, light ground contact, and rapid cadence (180 spm).
- *Direct Brick Workout (Bike to Run)*: Bike ride (e.g. 60–90km at 70–75% FTP) immediately followed by a rapid 5-minute transition (T2) into a 5km run starting at goal race pace to drill heavy-leg motor recruitment.

### 4. Intra-Workout Fueling & Nutrition Strategy
- In every session's `details`, state specific fueling guidance (e.g., "Sip 500ml fluid with 40–60g maltodextrin/fructose mix and 500mg sodium per hour on the bike", or "Consume 1 gel (25g carbs) at 45 mins with 200ml water").
